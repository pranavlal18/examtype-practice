ExamType Practice Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 8, 2021

Last updated: May 12, 2025

ExamType Practice ("ExamType Practice", "we", "us", "our") is a free, static typing practice tool for Indian government competitive exams. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled while you use the site.

ExamType Practice is designed to be as private as possible. It runs as a static site with no backend, no accounts, and no third-party tracking, advertising, or crash-reporting services. This Privacy Policy describes what data we handle, how it is stored, and your choices.

Under this Privacy Policy, ExamType Practice handles only data that is stored locally in your browser, as described below. Our contact information can be found at the end of this Privacy Policy. ExamType Practice is not affiliated with SSC, RRB, Indian Railways, Government of India, or Monkeytype.

What data do we collect?

ExamType Practice does not collect personal data and does not use accounts. We do not collect your email address, username, real name, or any other personally identifiable information.

The only information the application works with is stored locally in your browser, in localStorage: your typing results, settings, and preferences you choose. This data never leaves your browser.

How do we handle and store your data?

All practice data is stored locally in your browser through localStorage. There is no account system, no sync, and no backend database that ExamType Practice uses to store or process your data. Clearing your browser storage removes the data.

Advertising, analytics, and crash reporting

ExamType Practice does not use advertising networks, analytics services, crash reporting, or any other third-party data collection. No such data is collected, and no such services are loaded by the site.

What are cookies?

ExamType Practice does not use tracking or advertising cookies. It may use browser localStorage for preferences and test data, which is under your control.

How to manage your data

Because your data stays in your browser, you can clear or export it at any time using your browser settings. There is no account or server-side copy to manage.

Hosting and infrastructure

ExamType Practice is deployed as a static website. The static hosting provider may generate standard server logs, such as IP addresses, browser user agents, and timestamps, for security and operational purposes. These logs are not used for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

Privacy policies of other websites

ExamType Practice contains links to other external websites, such as the source code repository on GitHub. This Privacy Policy only applies to ExamType Practice, so if you click to another website, you should read that website's privacy policy.

Changes to our privacy policy

ExamType Practice may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are made through the source code repository, and the latest version is published on this page.

How to contact us

If you have any questions about ExamType Practice's privacy policy, the data we hold on you, or you would like to exercise one of your data protection rights, please do not hesitate to contact us.
